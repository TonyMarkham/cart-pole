How to read this

What you see. The camera's full picture halved to 728 by 544 pixels in greyscale, the frames the detector and the calibration use, taken with the same shutter and gain ([stream] in the Orin's settings.toml). Pixel positions count from the picture's top left corner. The picture here changes a few times a second, but the detector looks at every frame the camera sends, up to 60 a second.

Found lately. In how many of the latest frames each marker was found: green when it was found in every one, orange when it was missed in any. A marker that flickers between found and missed is at the edge of what the detector can see, and is worth moving further into the picture.

Aiming for the calibration. The calibration measures the pole hanging at each end and at the centre, held level pointing away from the centre at each end, and held straight up at each end. With the cart at an end, hold the pole level and check that End is found in every frame; then do the same at the other end.

The camera is shared. The live view and a calibration measurement use the same camera, so a measurement stops this view, and this view cannot start while a measurement runs. The view stops by itself when no page has looked at it for a few seconds ([live] idle_stop_s), so a closed page never keeps the camera from the calibration.
