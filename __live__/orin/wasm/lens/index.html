Measure the lens

The lens bends the picture, most at its edges, and the calibration's matrix cannot follow a bend. This measures the bend with the printed board: hold the board about 1 m from the camera, over the dashed shape inside the red box, until the box turns green and stays green for a moment. That still is kept and the box moves on by itself: first across the whole picture with the board flat, then with the board turned. Then press Solve.

Asking the Orin.

How to read this

The dashed shape is the board as the camera sees it 1 m away, worked out on the camera's own scale, measured from the board: its 32.25 mm markers measured 18.5 pixels at 2 m, so at 1 m a pixel is 32.25 ÷ 18.5 × ½ = 0.872 mm, and the 258 × 172 mm of squares are 296 × 197 pixels. The red box is 20% larger, 355 × 237 pixels, so a hand-held board fits inside it with room (box_margin; [lens] and [lens_board] in the Orin's settings.toml).

The positions. The first 25 tile the whole picture row by row from the top left, with the board flat, facing the camera, so the stills reach every edge and corner, where the lens bends most. The next 20 put the box at the centre and at each corner, and ask for the board turned 30° with its left, right, top or bottom side, as the picture shows it, farther from the camera: the dashed shape is drawn turned, with that side shorter. Turned stills are needed because stills of a flat board, wherever it is held, all tell OpenCV the same thing about the lens (Zhang, 2000, the method calibrateCamera is built on). Skip this position moves on when the board cannot be held there.

Green. The yellow outlines are the board's markers the camera finds, and the orange line is the board's edge they put it at. The box turns green when at least 11 of the board's 15 corners are found and everything found is inside the box, and, at a turned position, the named side is turned at least 20° away (min_tilt_deg). One missed marker costs at most 4 corners, so it does not stop a still being kept. The still is kept once the box has stayed green for hold_frames frames in a row, so a board still moving into place is not kept.

The bars. Turn, at turned positions only, shows how far the board is turned now: full at the 30° aimed for, with a tick at the 20° that counts, green from there. It is worked out from the corners found with OpenCV's solvePnP, on the camera's scale measured from the board, so it does not depend on how far away the board is held. Aiming for 30° by eye, the board measured about 22–26°, which is why 20° counts. Held fills as the frames that count add up, and positions done counts the positions kept or skipped.

Save picture saves what the camera sees now on the Orin, with the box and the outlines drawn in, so it can be looked at from another room. It is for looking at only: it is not a kept still, and it does not move the box.

Nothing is lost. Each still is saved on the Orin as it is kept, and the next position is remembered, so a closed page or a restart carries on where it was. Start over forgets them all. The camera is shared: this view stops when the live view or a calibration measurement starts.

Solve. OpenCV's calibrateCamera works out the lens's centre, its focal lengths and its bend from the kept stills. The result is stored in lens.json on the Orin, with a dated copy in saved/. The calibration does not use it yet.
